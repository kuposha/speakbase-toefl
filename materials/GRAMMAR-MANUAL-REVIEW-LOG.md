## Review rules

A lesson can be marked COMPLETE only after:
- exact source unit/pages are opened and checked against the lesson scope;
- theory, forms, exceptions, examples and translations are reviewed;
- every closed-answer key is checked against the prompt and all distractors;
- every rationale and distractor explanation is checked;
- encoding and Markdown rendering are checked;
- source references and page numbers are accurate;
- the lesson is reviewed in the app and its learner-facing flow is tested.

Statuses:
- NOT_REVIEWED: no complete hands-on review in this pass.
- PARTIAL: a clearly identified subset has been reviewed or repaired; other gates remain open.
- COMPLETE: all review gates above have evidence.

Do not infer completion from automated structural tests or source navigation checks.
