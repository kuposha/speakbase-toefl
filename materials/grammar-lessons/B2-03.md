## 4. Полный конспект

### 4.1. Основная идея и функция конструкции

В modal + have + V3 модальный глагол передаёт отношение говорящего, а have + V3 относит событие к прошлому. May have left — возможно, ушла; must have left — судя по данным, наверняка ушла; can't have left — уход исключён.

### 4.2. Формирование и грамматические модели

Для упрёка выбери should have + V3, если контекст показывает упущенную обязанность. Needn't have paid сообщает, что человек заплатил зря. Didn't need to pay сообщает лишь об отсутствии необходимости: факт оплаты надо узнать из контекста.

### 4.3. Как выбрать подходящую форму

В пассиве между have и причастием появляется been: might have been moved. Не путай запрет mustn't с отрицательным выводом can't have. Could have в зависимости от контекста допускает предположение или неиспользованный шанс.

### 4.4. Сравнение с близкими конструкциями

| Form | Meaning | Contrast |
|---|---|---|
| could speak five languages | general past ability | was able to escape = specific successful achievement |
| could have stayed | past opportunity that was not taken | might have stayed = possible past action |
| must have left | strong positive deduction | might have left = uncertain possibility |
| can't have received | strong negative deduction | might not have received = perhaps did not receive |
| mustn't tell | prohibition | needn't tell = no necessity |
| needn't have booked | booked, but it was unnecessary | didn't need to book = no necessity; occurrence not specified |
| should have called | past criticism/expectation | had better call = strong advice now/for a specific situation |
| should review / should have reviewed | advice now / unfulfilled past expectation | must review = stronger obligation |
| might as well go | no better alternative | may go = possible choice |

### 4.5. Примеры с переводом и объяснением

| English | Перевод | Почему это подходит |
|---|---|---|---|
| `You needn't have brought a printed copy; the digital version was enough.` | Тебе не нужно было приносить распечатанную копию: цифровой версии было достаточно. | Needn't have + V3 means the action happened but was unnecessary. |
| `The results are inconsistent. It is possible that the team may have entered one value incorrectly.` | Результаты противоречивы. Возможно, команда неправильно ввела одно из значений; это одно из возможных объяснений. | May have expresses a possible explanation without claiming certainty. |
| `You should have told me earlier; I could have helped.` | Тебе следовало сказать мне раньше — я мог(ла) бы помочь. | Should have + V3 expresses criticism or an unrealised advisable past action. |
| `The parcel isn't here. It might have been delivered to the wrong address.` | Посылки здесь нет. Возможно, её доставили не по тому адресу. | Might have been delivered is a possibility about a past passive event. |

Примеры составлены Speakbase. Они иллюстрируют грамматическую функцию, но не подменяют индивидуальную проверку всех возможных вариантов и исключений.

### 4.6. Исключения, ограничения и границы употребления

- Could is normally used for general past ability; for a single successful achievement, use was/were able to or managed to.
- Must/can't as deductions are different from must/have to as obligation; context determines the meaning.
- Mustn't means prohibition, not lack of necessity. Use needn't/don't have to for absence of necessity.
- Needn't have + V3 presupposes that the action happened; didn't need to does not specify whether it happened.
- May/might not express uncertain negative possibility; can't/couldn't have can express strong negative deduction. **Couldn't** may also express inability, depending on context.
- Should have can express criticism/regret or an unfulfilled expectation; must have usually expresses a strong deduction about the past.
- Had better is usually tied to a specific situation and possible consequence; should is broader and less forceful.
- The mandative subjunctive uses the base form without third-person -s; do not add to after suggest in the pattern suggest that + clause.
- Might as well is an idiom of practical choice, not a simple synonym of may.
- Would has several meanings (hypothetical, reported will, refusal, repeated past action); do not assign one meaning to every occurrence.

### 4.7. Типичные ошибки русскоязычных студентов

| `You mustn't have brought a printed copy; the digital version was enough.` | `You needn't have brought a printed copy; the digital version was enough.` | Mustn't have does not express lack of necessity in this sense. |
| `The results are inconsistent. It is possible that the team must to have entered one value incorrectly.` | `The results are inconsistent. It is possible that the team may have entered one value incorrectly.` | To is not used after modal may. |
| `You should to have told me earlier; I could have helped.` | `You should have told me earlier; I could have helped.` | To is not used after should. |

Русский перевод помогает понять смысл, но не управляет английской формой напрямую. Проверяй роль каждого элемента и грамматическую модель английского предложения.

### 4.8. Произношение и восприятие на слух

Modal auxiliaries are commonly unstressed and reduced in connected speech; their full forms may become prominent under contrast or emphasis. Listen for the distinction between **must have** and **might have**, and for the weak auxiliary sequence in **should have**. Sentence stress can strengthen or soften a deduction. Phonetic transcriptions and audio examples still require dictionary-audio verification.

Для аудирования распознавай слабые формы вспомогательных глаголов, окончания и границы смысловых групп. Не ориентируйся только на чтение по буквам.

### 4.12. Краткая памятка

- Ability: can (present/general), could (general past), was able to/managed to (specific past success).
- Deduction: must (strong positive), can't/couldn't (strong negative); past = must/can't have + V3.
- Possibility: may/might/could; past = may/might/could have + V3.
- Obligation: must/have to; prohibition = mustn't; no necessity = needn't/don't have to.
- Needn't have done = done but unnecessary; didn't need to = no necessity, occurrence unspecified.
- Advice: should/ought to; past = should have + V3; urgent specific advice = had better.
- Formal that-clause: should + base verb or mandative subjunctive base form.
- Would can be hypothetical, reported, refusal or repeated past action; use context.
