## 4. Полный конспект

### 4.1. Основная идея и функция конструкции

Reporting verbs encode not only speech but also the speaker's stance and the structure of reported content. Different verbs govern different complements: gerund, that-clause, object + infinitive, or object + preposition + gerund.

Тема этого блока — **Reporting verbs and reporting structures**. Измеримая цель: Передавать позицию автора, выбирать reporting verb и модели с that/to/-ing

### 4.2. Формирование и грамматические модели

**1. Direct and reported speech**

Direct speech quotes the original words. Reported speech gives the content from a later reporting point:
- Direct: *Paul said, "I am feeling ill."*
- Reported: *Paul said (that) he was feeling ill.*

When the reporting verb is past, backshift is common: present => past; present perfect => past perfect; will => would; can => could. The pronouns and time/place expressions change only as required by the new context.

**2. When backshift is not necessary**

If the reported situation is still true or relevant, the present can remain:
- *Helen told me that she wants to go to Canada next year.*

If the situation has changed or is no longer true, use the past form:
- *Rachel said Joe was in hospital* (but he is no longer there).

The past simple in the reported clause can sometimes remain past simple or move to past perfect; choose according to clarity and the time relationship.

**3. Say and tell**

- **Say + (that) clause**: *She said (that) she was tired.*
- **Tell + object + (that) clause**: *She told me (that) she was tired.*
- **Say something to somebody**: *She said goodbye to me.*

Do not use *say me* or *tell that* when a listener is specified without an object.

**4. Reported instructions, requests and questions**

- **Tell/ask + object + (not) to-infinitive**: *The doctor told me to rest; I asked him not to leave.* The object identifies the person receiving the instruction or request.
- Yes/no question: **ask + if/whether + statement order**: *'Are you ready?' => She asked whether I was ready.*
- Wh-question: **ask + wh-word + statement order**: *'Where do you live?' => He asked where I lived.*

Do not keep question inversion or do/does/did in the reported question. The reported clause is not itself a question, so it normally has no question-mark punctuation unless the whole reporting sentence is a direct question.

**5. Reporting verbs + -ing / that-clause**

- **Suggest/recommend + -ing** or **that + subject + (should) + base verb**: *They recommended revising the draft / that we (should) revise the draft.* In the that-clause, should is optional in many varieties; the bare subjunctive is also possible.
- **Admit/deny + -ing** or **that-clause**. In a different meaning, **admit someone to/into a place** means allow them to enter; this is not the same reporting pattern as **admit + -ing/that-clause**.
- **Consider/avoid/finish/risk + -ing** where the verb requires it.
- **Suggest** does not normally take **object + to-infinitive**: *She suggested that I ask*, not *suggested me to ask*.

**6. Reporting verbs + to-infinitive**

- **Agree/offer/promise/refuse/decide + to-infinitive**: *She promised to return.* The understood subject of the infinitive is normally the subject of the reporting verb.
- **Claim/seem/appear + to-infinitive**; use **to have + V3** when the reported event is earlier than the reporting point: *He claimed to have seen the document.*
- **Tell/advise/warn/encourage/persuade + object + to-infinitive**. The object is the person expected or encouraged to act.
- Negative instruction: **warn/tell/advise + object + not to-infinitive**. With **warn**, *warn someone against doing something* is another common pattern.

**7. Verb + preposition + -ing**

- **Accuse someone of doing**, **congratulate someone on doing**, **prevent/stop someone from doing**, **suspect someone of doing**, **thank someone for doing**.
- **Insist on doing**, **apologise for doing**, **look forward to doing**, **succeed in doing**.
- The preposition is part of the verb pattern; after a preposition, use -ing, not a to-infinitive.

**8. Reporting stance and source attribution**

Reporting verbs signal whether a source claims, admits, denies, suggests, recommends, warns or argues. Choose a verb that accurately represents the source's position and strength. Do not upgrade a suggestion to proof or a report to a confirmed fact.

### 4.3. Как выбрать подходящую форму

1. Decide whether you are quoting exact words or reporting their content.
2. Identify the reporting verb and whether backshift is required by the time/context.
3. Check pronoun and time/place changes; do not change them mechanically if the reference point is unchanged.
4. Choose say + clause, tell + object + clause, or say + words + to + person.
5. For a reported question, use statement word order and the correct wh-/if/whether marker.
6. For instructions/requests, use tell/ask + object + (not) to-infinitive.
7. Look up the reporting verb's complement: -ing, that-clause, to-infinitive, object + to-infinitive or preposition + -ing.
8. Preserve the preposition in patterns such as accuse of, insist on and prevent from.
9. Match the reporting verb to the source's actual stance and evidence.

### 4.4. Сравнение с близкими конструкциями

She suggested changing the plan. He denied taking the file. They accused him of copying the answer. The tutor encouraged us to revise.

| Что проверяем | Вопрос для выбора |
|---|---|
| Форма | Какие элементы обязательны и в каком порядке они стоят? |
| Значение | Что именно меняется: время, длительность, реальность, степень уверенности или фокус? |
| Контекст | Что уже известно собеседнику и каков регистр высказывания? |
| Проверка | Если заменить форму близкой конструкцией, изменится ли смысл? Объясни, почему. |

### 4.5. Примеры с переводом и объяснением

| English | Перевод | Почему это подходит |
|---|---|---|---|
| `The committee suggested extending the deadline.` | Комитет предложил продлить срок. | Suggest commonly takes a gerund or a that-clause; here the gerund is used. |
| `The student denied copying the confidential file.` | Студент отрицал, что скопировал конфиденциальный файл. | Deny is followed by a gerund when referring to an action. |
| `The journalist accused the official of misleading the public.` | Журналист обвинил чиновника в том, что тот вводил общественность в заблуждение. | Accuse + object + of + V-ing is the standard pattern. |
| `The supervisor encouraged the interns to ask questions.` | Руководитель поощрял стажёров задавать вопросы. | Encourage + object + to-infinitive: encouraged the interns to ask. |

Примеры составлены Speakbase. Они иллюстрируют грамматическую функцию, но не подменяют индивидуальную проверку всех возможных вариантов и исключений.

### 4.6. Исключения, ограничения и границы употребления

- Backshift is common, not automatic; a fact that remains true/current can stay in the present.
- Past simple can sometimes remain past simple or become past perfect; use context to show sequence.
- Say does not take a listener as a direct object (*say me*); tell normally needs an object when the listener is specified.
- Reported questions use statement order, not direct-question inversion.
- Suggest/recommend do not normally take object + to-infinitive; use -ing or a that-clause.
- Tell/ask/warn/advise + object + to-infinitive requires the object when the person is specified.
- Verbs such as admit/deny can take -ing or a that-clause; choose the structure that preserves the intended meaning.
- Preposition + -ing patterns are lexical: accuse of, insist on, prevent from, apologise for, thank for. Do not replace the preposition with a guessed alternative.
- Reporting language attributes a claim; it does not prove the claim true.

### 4.7. Типичные ошибки русскоязычных студентов

| `The committee suggested to extend the deadline.` | `The committee suggested extending the deadline.` | To extend does not fit the selected suggest + V-ing pattern. |
| `The student denied to copy the confidential file.` | `The student denied copying the confidential file.` | To copy is not the standard complement after deny. |
| `The journalist accused the official for misleading the public.` | `The journalist accused the official of misleading the public.` | For does not govern the complement after accuse in this structure. |

Русский перевод помогает понять смысл, но не управляет английской формой напрямую. Проверяй роль каждого элемента и грамматическую модель английского предложения.

### 4.10. Примеры для управляемого разбора

- The committee suggested ___ the deadline. => `extending` (not *extend* or *to extend* in this pattern).
- The student denied ___ the confidential file. => `copying` (not *copy* or *to copy*).
- The journalist accused the official ___ misleading the public. => `of` (accuse someone of doing something).

Для каждого примера назови целевой элемент, объясни форму, укажи, почему отвлекающий вариант неверен, и придумай собственное предложение с тем же правилом.

### 4.11. Самопроверка

Перед отправкой ответа проверь:
1. Правильно ли определена функция конструкции?
2. Согласована ли форма с подлежащим и временной перспективой?
3. Не перенесён ли русский порядок слов напрямую?
4. Не смешаны ли две похожие модели?
5. Не сильнее ли утверждение, чем позволяют контекст и доказательства?

### 4.12. Краткая памятка

- Direct speech quotes; reported speech adapts pronouns, tense and time/place reference.
- Backshift is common after a past reporting verb, but current truths may remain present.
- Say + clause; tell + object + clause; say something to somebody.
- Reported questions = wh-/if/whether + statement word order.
- Instructions = tell/ask + object + (not) to-infinitive.
- Suggest/recommend + -ing or that-clause; admit/deny + -ing or that-clause.
- Promise/offer/refuse/agree + to-infinitive; persuade/encourage/warn/tell + object + to-infinitive.
- Accuse of / insist on / prevent from / apologise for + -ing.
- Choose reporting verbs that accurately represent source stance and evidence.
