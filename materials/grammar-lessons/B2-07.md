## 4. Полный конспект

### 4.1. Основная идея и функция конструкции

Относительное придаточное отвечает либо на вопрос «который именно?», либо добавляет сведения об уже определённом предмете. Запятые меняют объём группы: guests who paid — заплатившие гости; the guests, who paid, — заплатили все названные гости.

### 4.2. Формирование и грамматические модели

Whose + существительное указывает на владельца. После вынесенного предлога в формальном стиле выбирай whom для людей и which для вещей: to whom, in which. В разговорном варианте предлог можно оставить в конце.

### 4.3. Как выбрать подходящую форму

В определительном придаточном иногда опускается объектное местоимение, но не подлежащее. Сокращённое visitors waiting передаёт активное действие, forms signed by Jo — действие над формами. Which может отсылать и ко всему событию.

### 4.4. Сравнение с близкими конструкциями

| Form | Example | Difference |
|---|---|---|
| defining | The researcher who designed the test received an award. | Identifies which researcher; no commas |
| non-defining | Dr Lee, who designed the test, received an award. | Adds extra information; commas; no that |
| object relative omitted | The paper (that) I read was useful. | Relative pronoun is object |
| subject relative | The paper that explains the method is useful. | Relative pronoun is subject; cannot omit |
| fronted preposition | the consultant to whom I wrote | Formal; preposition before whom |
| stranded preposition | the consultant (who/that) I wrote to | Neutral/informal |
| clause-level which | The trial failed, which surprised the team. | Which refers to the whole preceding event |
| reduced relative | the devices installed last year | Passive V3 phrase; devices receive the action |

### 4.5. Примеры с переводом и объяснением

| English | Перевод | Почему это подходит |
|---|---|---|---|
| `The framework within which the study was conducted was established earlier.` | Концептуальная основа, в рамках которой проводилось исследование, была разработана ранее. | Which refers to the framework after a fronted preposition within. |
| `The consultant to whom we sent the report replied immediately.` | Консультант, которому мы отправили отчёт, ответил немедленно. | Whom is the formal object form referring to a person after a preposition. |
| `The survey included five hundred participants, many of whom were international students.` | В опросе участвовали пятьсот человек, многие из которых были иностранными студентами. | Whom refers to people after many of. |
| `The devices damaged during the trial are now being inspected.` | Устройства, повреждённые во время испытания, сейчас проходят осмотр. | Damaged is a passive participle: the devices received the action. |

Примеры составлены Speakbase. Они иллюстрируют грамматическую функцию, но не подменяют индивидуальную проверку всех возможных вариантов и исключений.

### 4.6. Исключения, ограничения и границы употребления

- Do not omit a relative pronoun when it is the subject of its clause.
- Object pronoun omission is possible in defining clauses in appropriate positions; non-defining clauses retain the pronoun.
- Do not use that in non-defining relative clauses or directly after a fronted preposition.
- Whom is formal; in everyday English, a stranded preposition with who/that or no pronoun is usually more natural.
- What means 'the thing(s) that' and cannot normally follow an antecedent noun such as everything or the thing.
- Which can refer to a whole preceding clause; what cannot.
- Whose is not limited to people; it can refer to organisations/things when possession is intended.
- Use where for place and when for time; do not use them as generic substitutes for which.
- Quantifier + of whom/which must have a clear plural antecedent.
- Reduced participle phrases need an unambiguous logical head; dangling or misplaced modifiers change the meaning.
- Non-defining clauses require commas; punctuation can change whether the information is identifying or additional.

### 4.7. Типичные ошибки русскоязычных студентов

| `The framework within who the study was conducted was established earlier.` | `The framework within which the study was conducted was established earlier.` | Who refers to people. |
| `The consultant to which we sent the report replied immediately.` | `The consultant to whom we sent the report replied immediately.` | Which refers to things, not the consultant. |
| `The survey included five hundred participants, many of which were international students.` | `The survey included five hundred participants, many of whom were international students.` | Which refers to things. |

Русский перевод помогает понять смысл, но не управляет английской формой напрямую. Проверяй роль каждого элемента и грамматическую модель английского предложения.

### 4.10. Примеры для управляемого разбора

- The framework within ___ the study was conducted was established earlier. => `which` (not *whose*).
- The consultant to ___ we sent the report replied immediately. => `whom` (not *whose*).
- The survey included five hundred participants, many of ___ were international students. => `whom` (not *whose*).

Для каждого примера назови целевой элемент, объясни форму, укажи, почему отвлекающий вариант неверен, и придумай собственное предложение с тем же правилом.

### 4.11. Самопроверка

Перед отправкой ответа проверь:
1. Правильно ли определена функция конструкции?
2. Согласована ли форма с подлежащим и временной перспективой?
3. Не перенесён ли русский порядок слов напрямую?
4. Не смешаны ли две похожие модели?
5. Не сильнее ли утверждение, чем позволяют контекст и доказательства?

### 4.12. Краткая памятка

- Defining = identifies the noun, no commas; non-defining = extra information, commas, no that and no omission.
- Relative pronoun as subject cannot be omitted; object may be omitted in defining clauses.
- Who/whom = people; which = things/whole clause; whose = possession; where/when/why = place/time/reason.
- Formal: preposition + whom/which; neutral: preposition can remain at the end.
- Quantifier + of whom/which refers to a plural set.
- What = the thing(s) that; never use after an antecedent noun.
- Reduced relative: -ing active, V3 passive; check the logical head and ambiguity.
