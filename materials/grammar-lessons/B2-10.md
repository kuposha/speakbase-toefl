## 4. Полный конспект

### 4.1. Основная идея и функция конструкции

В сложных предложениях форма сказуемого определяется грамматическим ядром subject phrase и конструкцией. Длинные modifiers, of-phrases и coordinated subjects могут отвлекать от head noun.

Тема этого блока — **Subject–verb agreement in complex structures**. Измеримая цель: Согласовывать сказуемое с реальным ядром подлежащего, включая сложные noun phrases и коллективные существительные

### 4.2. Формирование и грамматические модели

**1. Identify the grammatical subject and its head**

The verb agrees with the head of the subject, not with a nearby noun in a prepositional phrase or relative clause:
- *The quality of the reports is improving.*
- *The reports that the committee received are incomplete.*

**2. Compound subjects with and**

Two subjects joined by **and** normally take a plural verb:
- *The director and the researcher are present.*

Do not confuse a true coordinated subject with an added phrase such as **as well as, along with, together with**: these do not normally make the grammatical subject plural (*The director, along with the researchers, is present*).

A singular verb is possible when the two words express one combined idea/unit or a single dish/activity: *Fish and chips is my favourite meal.* Interpret the meaning, not just the conjunction.

**3. Either/or, neither/nor and not only/but also**

With **either...or, neither...nor** and **not only...but also**, the verb often agrees with the nearer subject. If the two subjects differ greatly in number, rewriting the sentence is often clearer than forcing proximity agreement:
- *Either the teacher or the students are responsible.*
- *Neither the students nor the teacher is available.*

When the subjects differ in number/person, rewrite if the nearest-subject agreement sounds awkward.

**4. Each, every, everyone, no one and none**

- **Each/every + singular noun** takes a singular verb: *Every student has a login.*
- **Everyone, somebody, nobody, no one** are grammatically singular: *Everyone is ready.*
- **Each of / every one of + plural noun/pronoun** normally takes a singular verb in formal standard usage. In a relative clause, identify its own antecedent: *one of the reports that were published* (that = reports), but *the only one of the reports that was published* (that = one).
- **None of** can take singular or plural agreement depending on the noun and intended meaning; singular is common with an uncountable/singular idea, plural is natural with plural count nouns.

**5. Collective nouns**

Collective nouns (team, family, government, committee, staff, company) can take singular or plural verbs:
- singular when the group is treated as one unit;
- plural when attention is on the individuals within the group.
British English uses plural collective agreement more readily than American English. Keep pronouns consistent with the chosen viewpoint.

**6. Nouns that look plural or singular**

- Plural-only in their ordinary meanings: *the police, people, cattle, trousers, scissors, glasses* => plural verb. Use a singular count noun for one member/item (*a police officer, a person, a pair of scissors*).
- Usually singular despite -s: *news, mathematics, economics, physics, athletics*.
- *Series, species, means* can be singular or plural depending on number.
- Company/team names may take singular or plural according to the variety and whether the organisation/team is treated as a unit or members. **Data** is traditionally plural in formal scientific writing (*the data are*), but is also widely treated as a mass singular in general usage (*the data is*); follow the chosen style consistently.

**7. Amounts, measurements, percentages and fractions**

- A sum, period, distance or quantity treated as one unit takes singular agreement: *Ten years is a long time.*
- With a percentage/fraction, agreement depends on the noun after **of**: *40% of the land is...; 40% of the students are...*
- A number of + plural noun takes plural; the number of + plural noun takes singular: *A number of issues remain; the number of issues is increasing.*

**8. Uncountable and plural-only nouns**

Uncountable nouns such as information, advice, equipment and furniture take singular agreement. Do not add plural -s or use a plural verb unless the noun is rephrased as countable units.

### 4.3. Как выбрать подходящую форму

1. Find the full subject and identify its head noun.
2. Ignore intervening prepositional phrases and relative clauses when locating the head.
3. With and, decide whether the subject is two separate items or one combined idea.
4. With either/or, neither/nor and not only/but also, check the nearer subject and rewrite if the result is awkward.
5. Treat each/every/everyone/no one as singular; check the structure after each of/every one of.
6. Decide whether a collective noun refers to a unit or its members and keep pronouns consistent.
7. Check plural-only nouns, singular -s nouns, and singular/plural forms such as series/species/means.
8. With amounts and percentages, identify whether the expression is one unit and inspect the noun after of.
9. Distinguish a number of (plural) from the number of (singular).

### 4.4. Сравнение с близкими конструкциями

The number of applicants is rising. A number of applicants are waiting. Neither the manager nor the assistants were available (nearest subject plural).

| Что проверяем | Вопрос для выбора |
|---|---|
| Форма | Какие элементы обязательны и в каком порядке они стоят? |
| Значение | Что именно меняется: время, длительность, реальность, степень уверенности или фокус? |
| Контекст | Что уже известно собеседнику и каков регистр высказывания? |
| Проверка | Если заменить форму близкой конструкцией, изменится ли смысл? Объясни, почему. |

### 4.5. Примеры с переводом и объяснением

| English | Перевод | Почему это подходит |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Neither the director nor the assistants were available.` | Ни директор, ни ассистенты не были доступны. | With neither...nor, the verb commonly agrees with the nearer subject assistants (plural). |
| `Not only the students but also the lecturer is responsible for the results.` | Не только студенты, но и преподаватель ответственен за эти результаты. | With not only...but also, agreement follows the nearer subject lecturer (singular). |
| `The number of applications has increased this year.` | В этом году количество заявок увеличилось. | The head is number (singular), so use has. |
| `A number of applications are still incomplete.` | Некоторые заявки всё ещё не заполнены. | A number of + plural noun means several and normally takes plural agreement. |

Примеры составлены Speakbase. Они иллюстрируют грамматическую функцию, но не подменяют индивидуальную проверку всех возможных вариантов и исключений.

### 4.6. Исключения, ограничения и границы употребления

- Do not let a nearby noun inside an of-phrase or relative clause control the verb; identify the head.
- And normally creates a plural subject, but one combined idea can be singular.
- With either/or and neither/nor, nearest-subject agreement is common; rewrite mismatched subjects if needed.
- Each/every/everyone/no one are singular; each of/every one of normally takes singular formal agreement.
- None of can be singular or plural according to the noun and meaning; do not state an absolute rule.
- Collective noun agreement varies by dialect and meaning; British and American usage can differ.
- Police, people and several clothing/tool nouns are plural; news and many -ics subjects are singular.
- Series/species/means have identical singular and plural forms; determine number from context.
- A percentage/fraction agrees with the noun after of; amounts/time/distance may be singular when viewed as one unit.
- A number of + plural noun differs from the number of + plural noun.

### 4.7. Типичные ошибки русскоязычных студентов

| `Neither the director nor the assistants was available.` | `Neither the director nor the assistants were available.` | Was incorrectly agrees with the more distant singular noun director. |
| `Not only the students but also the lecturer are responsible for the results.` | `Not only the students but also the lecturer is responsible for the results.` | Are incorrectly agrees with the more distant plural noun students. |
| `The number of applications have increased this year.` | `The number of applications has increased this year.` | Have incorrectly agrees with applications inside the of-phrase. |

Русский перевод помогает понять смысл, но не управляет английской формой напрямую. Проверяй роль каждого элемента и грамматическую модель английского предложения.

### 4.10. Примеры для управляемого разбора

- Neither the director nor the assistants ___ available. => `were` (nearer subject: assistants).
- Not only the students but also the lecturer ___ responsible for the results. => `is` (nearer subject: lecturer).
- The number of applications ___ increased this year. => `has` (head noun: number).

Для каждого примера назови целевой элемент, объясни форму, укажи, почему отвлекающий вариант неверен, и придумай собственное предложение с тем же правилом.

### 4.12. Краткая памятка

- Verb agrees with the head of the subject, not the nearest noun.
- Two subjects + and => usually plural; one combined idea can be singular.
- Either/or, neither/nor, not only/but also => often nearest-subject agreement.
- Each/every/everyone/no one => singular; none of depends on meaning.
- Collective nouns: singular as a unit, plural as members; dialect matters.
- Police/people/trousers/scissors => plural; news/physics/economics => singular.
- Series/species/means can be singular or plural.
- Amounts/time/distance often singular as one unit; percentages agree with the noun after of.
- A number of + plural; the number of + singular.
