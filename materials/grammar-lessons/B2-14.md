## 3. Место темы и границы

Distinguish concession (although, even though, despite, concessive while/whilst) from contrast between two situations (whereas/contrastive while); separately distinguish an accepted fact from a hypothetical condition (even though vs even if).

## 4. Полный конспект

### 4.1. Основная идея и функция конструкции

Уступка признаёт препятствие и сохраняет основной вывод. Although it rained, we played сообщает два факта. Even if it rains, we will play оставляет дождь возможным. Whereas обычно противопоставляет характеристики двух объектов.

### 4.2. Формирование и грамматические модели

После although идёт придаточное, после despite — существительное, -ing или the fact that + придаточное. Связующее however не соединяет две личные части одной запятой. Поставь точку либо точку с запятой перед ним.

### 4.3. Как выбрать подходящую форму

While может обозначать время или противопоставление. Проверь, происходят ли два действия одновременно или автор сравнивает признаки. Не дублируй although и but для одной и той же связи.

### 4.4. Сравнение с близкими конструкциями

**Concession with an accepted fact:** Although / Even though Matt does not speak Spanish, I think he should still visit Madrid.
**Hypothetical condition:** Even if Matt does not speak Spanish, I think he should still visit Madrid.
**Contrast between two situations:** Juan gets lots of homework from school, whereas Mia gets very little.
**Concessive while:** While the sample was small, the findings were consistent.
**Concessive adjective fronting:** Strange though it may seem, the result is consistent.

### 4.5. Примеры с переводом и объяснением

| English | Перевод | Почему это подходит |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Although the evidence is limited, the conclusion remains useful.` | Хотя доказательств мало, вывод остаётся полезным. | Although introduces a finite clause; the contrast is between limited evidence and a useful conclusion. |
| `Despite the high cost, the method was adopted widely.` | Несмотря на высокую стоимость, метод получил широкое распространение. | Despite is followed by a noun phrase, not a finite clause. |
| `However carefully the samples were stored, some uncertainty remained.` | Как бы тщательно ни хранились образцы, некоторая неопределённость сохранялась. | Advanced degree-concessive however; its source verification remains pending. |
| `Much as I respect the researcher, I disagree with this interpretation.` | Как бы я ни уважал(а) исследователя, я не согласен/не согласна с этой интерпретацией. | Advanced concessive pattern; source verification remains pending. |
| `The method was expensive; nevertheless, it was adopted widely.` | Метод был дорогим; тем не менее его широко применяли. | Nevertheless is a conjunctive adverb linking independent clauses; use a semicolon or full stop before it and a comma after it. |
| `Even though Matt does not speak Spanish, I think he should still visit Madrid.` | Хотя Мэтт не говорит по-испански, я думаю, ему всё равно стоит посетить Мадрид. | Even though presents the lack of Spanish as a known fact. |
| `Even if Matt does not speak Spanish, I think he should still visit Madrid.` | Даже если Мэтт не говорит по-испански, я думаю, ему всё равно стоит посетить Мадрид. | Even if presents the situation as a condition or possibility. |
| `Juan gets lots of homework from school, whereas Mia gets very little.` | Хуан получает много домашних заданий в школе, тогда как Миа получает очень мало. | Whereas contrasts two situations; it does not express concession here. |
| `Strange though it may seem, the result is consistent.` | Как бы странно это ни казалось, результат остаётся непротиворечивым. | Emphatic adjective + though + subject + verb. |

Примеры составлены Speakbase. Они иллюстрируют грамматическую функцию, но не подменяют индивидуальную проверку всех возможных вариантов и исключений.

### 4.6. Исключения, ограничения и границы употребления

- Although/though/even though take a clause; despite/in spite of take a noun phrase or -ing, not a finite clause directly.
- Despite has no following of; use in spite of.
- Because/as/since/seeing that differ in information focus/register; do not replace them mechanically.
- In case is precaution, not a synonym of if; in case of takes a noun phrase.
- Unless already means except if/if not; avoid unintended double negatives.
- As and like differ by construction and register; like + clause is common informally, while as + clause is standard in formal writing.
- As if/as though can describe a real appearance or an unreal comparison; tense signals the intended distance.
- During takes a noun phrase; while/as take a clause. For expresses duration; by marks a deadline; until marks continuation. Concessive while expresses an unexpected contrast; contrastive while compares two situations.
- Do not use will in ordinary future time/condition clauses after when/until/as soon as/unless/provided.
- Hardly/scarcely pair with when; no sooner pairs with than.
- Purpose infinitives and result clauses are not interchangeable; check whether the clause states intention or consequence.

### 4.7. Типичные ошибки русскоязычных студентов

| `Despite the high cost, the method was adopted widely.` | Несмотря на высокую стоимость, метод получил широкое распространение. | Despite is followed by a noun phrase, not a finite clause. |
| `Despite the method was expensive, it was adopted widely.` | `Although the method was expensive, it was adopted widely.` | Despite cannot introduce a finite clause directly. |
| `However the method was expensive, it was adopted widely.` | `Although the method was expensive, it was adopted widely.` | However is a conjunctive adverb, not a subordinating conjunction; use although to introduce the finite concessive clause. |

Русский перевод помогает понять смысл, но не управляет английской формой напрямую. Проверяй роль каждого элемента и грамматическую модель английского предложения.

### 4.8. Произношение и восприятие на слух

Practise sentence stress in concessive clauses and the reduced pronunciation of although, despite and however in connected speech.

Для аудирования распознавай слабые формы вспомогательных глаголов, окончания и границы смысловых групп. Не ориентируйся только на чтение по буквам.

### 4.9. Академический английский и TOEFL-перенос

In academic reading, identify whether the linker introduces a clause or noun phrase and track contrast. In writing, use concession to qualify a claim without weakening its logic.

- **Reading:** синтаксические связи, reference, modifiers и логические отношения.
- **Listening:** tense/aspect, weak forms, modality, speaker stance и связи между идеями.
- **Writing — Build a Sentence:** точность структуры, согласование, порядок слов и verb patterns.
- **Writing — Email / Academic Discussion:** ясность, appropriate register, аргументация и cohesion.
- **Speaking — Listen and Repeat / Take an Interview:** точность воспроизведения формы и самостоятельное применение.

Это перенос навыка, а не полноценный экзаменационный пробник и не обещание конкретного балла.

### 4.10. Примеры для управляемого разбора

- We know that it rained a lot. ___ it rained a lot, they had a good time. (Although / Even if) Answer: Although.
- ___ the rain, they had a good time. (Despite / In spite of) Answer: In spite of.
- We know Matt does not speak Spanish. ___ Matt does not speak Spanish, he should still visit Madrid. (Even though / Even if) Answer: Even though.
- We do not know whether Matt speaks Spanish. ___ he does not speak Spanish, he should still visit Madrid. (Even though / Even if) Answer: Even if.
- The diesel model of the car is more expensive, but it is better value for money. Rewrite with while/whereas. Suggested answer: The diesel model of the car, while more expensive, is better value for money.
- ___ the diesel model is more expensive, it is better value for money. (Although / While) Answer: While.

Для каждого примера назови целевой элемент, объясни форму, укажи, почему отвлекающий вариант неверен, и придумай собственное предложение с тем же правилом.

5. Не сильнее ли утверждение, чем позволяют контекст и доказательства?

### 4.12. Краткая памятка

- Although/even though + clause; despite/in spite of + noun/-ing.
- Because/as/since + clause; because of + noun/-ing.
- In case = precaution; if = condition; unless = except if; provided/as long as = required condition.
- As + clause; like + noun (or informal clause); as if/as though = appearance/unreal comparison.
- During + noun; for + duration; while/as + clause; by = deadline; until = continuation.
- Future time/condition clauses take present tense, not will.
- Purpose: to/in order to/so as to; so that + clause.
- Result: so + adjective/adverb + that; such + noun phrase + that.
- Hardly/scarcely...when; no sooner...than; fronted negative/time expressions may trigger inversion. However/nevertheless/even so connect independent clauses and take sentence-level punctuation.
