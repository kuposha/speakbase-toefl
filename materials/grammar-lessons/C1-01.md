## 4. Полный конспект

### 4.1. Основная идея и функция конструкции

Negative inversion places an auxiliary before the subject after a fronted negative or restrictive expression. It creates formal emphasis and requires accurate auxiliary selection.

Главная функция — создать формальное логическое усиление через fronting; не используй инверсию ради усложнения.

### 4.2. Формирование и грамматическая модель

**Core patterns (keep the trigger and its fixed partner together):**
- Never / rarely / seldom / hardly ever + auxiliary + subject + main verb. Choose the auxiliary for tense/aspect: Never have we seen this; Rarely do they agree; Seldom will the committee reconsider.
- No sooner + had + subject + V3 + than + Past Simple clause.
- Hardly / scarcely / barely + had + subject + V3 + when/before + Past Simple clause.
- Only + time/adverbial/prepositional phrase + auxiliary + subject + main verb: Only later did she realise; Only after the data had been verified were the findings released.
- Not until + time/condition clause + auxiliary + subject + main verb in the main clause: Not until the train arrived did Miguel find his coat. Do not invert inside the not-until clause itself.
- Not only + auxiliary + subject + main verb, but (also) + a parallel second clause: Not only did Amy speak Japanese, but she could also write it.
- At no time / in no way / on no account / under no circumstances / not for one moment + auxiliary + subject + main verb.
- Little + auxiliary + subject + main verb expresses that someone does not know/expect something: Little did they know...
- So + adjective/adverb + be/auxiliary + subject + that-clause; such + be + noun phrase + that-clause.
- Neither / nor + auxiliary + subject adds a negative point to a previous clause.
- With a simple tense and no existing auxiliary, use do/does/did + subject + base verb. With be, a modal, a perfect or passive form, invert the first existing auxiliary.
- In formal negative inversion, do not use contracted negative forms (write had not, not hadn't).

Разбивай длинную конструкцию на части: subject, auxiliary/main verb, object/complement и обстоятельственные элементы. Не учи формулу без понимания роли каждой части. Если форма зависит от времени, числа, лица или регистра, фиксируй это явно.

### 4.3. Как выбрать подходящую форму

Алгоритм выбора:
1. Найди триггер в начале: negative adverbial, only + adverbial, not until, not only, little, so/such, neither/nor.
2. Проверь, какая часть предложения вынесена вперёд: она задаёт тип инверсии.
3. Найди первый auxiliary. Если его нет в Simple tense, добавь do/does/did и поставь main verb в base form.
4. Передвинь только первый auxiliary перед subject; не дублируй do с modal, be или have.
5. Проверь fixed pairs: no sooner...than; hardly/scarcely/barely...when/before; not only...but (also).
6. Если триггер not until / only after / only when, инверсия находится в main clause, а не во вложенной клаузе.
7. Если only относится к subject (Only Maria knew), инверсии нет; only + adverbial phrase требует инверсию (Only then did Maria know).
8. Не смешивай negative inversion с inverted conditionals (Had we known / Were it not for / Should you need): это отдельная тема C1-02.
### 4.4. Сравнение с близкими конструкциями

**Only + subject vs only + adverbial:** Only Maria knew the answer (no inversion); Only after the interview did Maria understand the answer (inversion in the main clause).
**Fixed pairs:** No sooner had the meeting begun than the alarm sounded; Hardly had the meeting begun when the alarm sounded.
**Negative inversion vs conditional inversion:** Never had we seen this (negative/restrictive trigger); Had we known, we would have acted (inverted conditional, a separate C1-02 pattern).

### 4.5. Примеры с переводом и объяснением

| English | Translation | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| Never have we collected such a comprehensive dataset. | Мы никогда не собирали настолько полный набор данных. | Never is fronted; the first auxiliary have moves before the subject, and the main verb remains V3. |
| No sooner had the meeting begun than the fire alarm sounded. | Едва началось собрание, как сработала пожарная сигнализация. | No sooner is paired with than; had + subject + V3 is followed by a Past Simple clause. |
| Hardly had they submitted the report when the editor requested revisions. | Едва они сдали отчёт, как редактор запросил правки. | Hardly is paired with when/before; do not swap this fixed partner with than. |
| Not until the final stage did the team recognise the scale of the risk. | Только на заключительном этапе команда осознала масштаб риска. | Not until is fronted; inversion occurs in the main clause (did + subject + base verb), not inside the time clause. |
| Not only did Amy speak Japanese fluently, but she could also write basic sentences. | Эми не только свободно говорила по-японски, но и могла писать простые предложения. | Not only triggers inversion in the first clause; the but-also clause keeps its own normal order. |
| Under no circumstances are passengers permitted to open the doors themselves. | Пассажирам ни при каких обстоятельствах не разрешается самостоятельно открывать двери. | Under no circumstances is a fronted negative prepositional phrase; be moves before the subject. |
| Little do they know how lucky they are. | Они даже не подозревают, как им повезло. | Little has a negative meaning and triggers do-support inversion. |
| So successful was her business that Lana was able to retire at the age of 50. | Её бизнес был настолько успешным, что Лана смогла выйти на пенсию в 50 лет. | So + adjective is fronted; be moves before the subject. |
| Such is the popularity of the play that the theatre is likely to be full every night. | Пьеса настолько популярна, что театр, вероятно, будет полон каждый вечер. | Such + be + noun phrase is an emphatic pattern. |
| For some time after the explosion Jack could not hear, and neither could he see. | Некоторое время после взрыва Джек не мог слышать, а также не мог видеть. | Neither adds a negative point and triggers auxiliary inversion. |
### 4.6. Исключения, ограничения и границы употребления

The trigger must be fronted and have the right function. Do not invert after every initial adverbial. Only + subject does not trigger inversion (Only Maria knew); only + time/adverbial phrase does (Only then did Maria know). With not until / only after / only when, invert the main clause, not the subordinate clause. No sooner pairs with than; hardly/scarcely/barely pair with when/before. Not only requires a parallel but (also) clause. In formal negative inversion, avoid contractions. Do not confuse negative inversion with conditional inversion (Had we known), which is covered separately in C1-02.

Не превращай наблюдение в абсолютное правило, если существуют варианты BrE/AmE, различия регистра или контекстные значения. В спорных случаях фиксируй конкретную норму и источник.

### 4.7. Типичные ошибки русскоязычных студентов

| Error | Correction | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Never we have collected such a comprehensive dataset. | Never have we collected such a comprehensive dataset. | Fronted never requires inversion of the first auxiliary. |
| No sooner did the meeting begun than the fire alarm sounded. | No sooner had the meeting begun than the fire alarm sounded. | No sooner pairs with than and uses had + subject + V3 in this pattern. |
| Hardly they had submitted the report when the editor requested revisions. | Hardly had they submitted the report when the editor requested revisions. | Hardly at the beginning requires subject-auxiliary inversion. |
| Only after the data had been verified the findings were released. | Only after the data had been verified were the findings released. | Only after triggers inversion in the main clause. |
| Not only Amy spoke Japanese fluently, but she could also write basic sentences. | Not only did Amy speak Japanese fluently, but she could also write basic sentences. | Not only requires inversion in the first clause and parallel structure in the second. |

### 4.10. Примеры для управляемого разбора

- Never ___ such a comprehensive dataset. Answer: have we collected. Why: fronted never triggers inversion; the first auxiliary moves before the subject.
- No sooner ___ the meeting begun than the fire alarm sounded. Answer: had. Why: no sooner is paired with than and uses Past Perfect inversion.
- Hardly ___ the report when the editor requested revisions. Answer: had they submitted. Why: hardly is paired with when/before.
- Not until the final stage ___ the scale of the risk. Answer: did the team recognise. Why: inversion belongs in the main clause.
- Not only ___ Japanese fluently, but she could also write basic sentences. Answer: did Amy speak. Why: not only triggers inversion in the first clause.
- Under no circumstances ___ passengers permitted to open the doors themselves. Answer: are. Why: invert be before the subject.
- Only after the data had been verified ___ released. Answer: were the findings. Why: only after triggers inversion in the main clause; invert the passive auxiliary before the subject.

### 4.12. Краткая памятка

- **Фокус:** Negative inversion and advanced inversion.
- **Базовая модель:** Never/Rarely/Seldom + auxiliary + subject + main verb. No sooner/Hardly + had + subject + V3 + than/when. Not until/Only after + clause/time phrase + did + subject + base verb.
- **Ключ выбора:** Определи negative/restrictive trigger и время clause. Затем перенеси auxiliary перед subject; если в Past Simple нет auxiliary, используй did + base verb.
- **Ограничение:** Inversion is triggered only by specific fronted expressions; ordinary initial adverbials do not always require it. Preserve tense and do not add two auxiliaries.
- При редактировании выбирай наиболее ясную и точную форму, а не самую сложную.
