## 4. Полный конспект

### 4.1. Основная идея и функция конструкции

Non-finite clauses compress information by using infinitives, gerunds and participles rather than a fully inflected finite verb. Their subject and time relationship must be clear from the main clause.

Тема этого блока — **Non-finite clauses and clause reduction**. Измеримая цель: Сокращать придаточные без двусмысленности; контролировать субъект и временные отношения

### 4.2. Формирование и грамматические модели

**1. Infinitive clauses for purpose or result**

- **to + infinitive** can express purpose: *To avoid confusion, label every sample.*
- Other infinitive clauses can complement adjectives/nouns or express a result; identify the function rather than assuming every infinitive is purpose.

**2. -ing clauses for simultaneous action, time or reason**

- *Kate was in the kitchen making coffee.*
- *Joe hurt his knee playing football.*
- *Feeling tired, I went to bed early.*

The -ing clause can describe an action happening at the same time, an accompanying circumstance or a reason. A clause at the beginning is usually followed by a comma.

**3. Earlier action: having + V3**

- *Having found a hotel, we looked for somewhere to eat.*
- *Having finished her work, she went home.*

Having + V3 marks an action completed before the main-clause action. It is common in written English. After + -ing can also express sequence: *After finishing her work, she went home.*

**4. Passive and reduced participle clauses**

- Active: *Police investigating the crime are looking for three men.*
- Passive: *The boy injured in the accident was taken to hospital.*
- Passive: *The gun used in the robbery has been found.*

Use -ing for an active relation and V3 for a passive relation. A reduced relative clause modifies the noun immediately before it.

**5. Perfect passive participle**

- *Having been warned about the danger, the visitors left.*

Having been + V3 marks an earlier passive event: the subject received the action before the main clause.

**6. Preposition + -ing**

After a preposition, use -ing:
- *By working carefully, the team reduced errors.*
- *Without being told, I would never have guessed.*
- *On returning home, she called the office.*

The preposition determines the relationship (method, absence, time, context). Do not confuse prepositional **to + -ing** (*look forward to going*) with infinitive **to + base verb**. **Avoid** is a lexical verb, not a preposition: use *avoid contamination* or *avoid contaminating the samples*; for purpose, use *to avoid contamination*, not *to avoid to contamination*.

**7. Conjunction + participle / reduced adverbial clause**

- *While waiting for the results, the team checked the data.*
- *Although criticised by reviewers, the paper was accepted.*
- *If asked, provide the original measurements.*

A conjunction can remain before a reduced clause when the subject is understood and the meaning remains clear. Use a full finite clause if the subject differs or the reduction is ambiguous.

**8. Subject control and absolute clauses**

The understood subject of a reduced adverbial clause normally matches the subject of the main clause:
- Correct: *Having checked the figures, the analyst submitted the report.*
- Incorrect: *Having checked the figures, the report was submitted.*

Some participle clauses can have their own explicit subject (absolute construction): *The weather being fine, we ate outside.* Use this only when the subject relationship is clear and the construction is appropriate to the register.

### 4.3. Как выбрать подходящую форму

1. Restore the full finite clause and identify who performs/receives the action.
2. For purpose, consider to + infinitive; for simultaneous/accompanying action, consider -ing.
3. For an earlier active action, use having + V3; for an earlier passive action, use having been + V3.
4. For a reduced passive relative/adverbial clause, use V3; for an active relation, use -ing.
5. After a preposition, use -ing; distinguish prepositional to from infinitive to.
6. Keep a conjunction before a reduced clause only when the understood subject is clear.
7. Compare the implied subject with the main-clause subject; if they differ, use a full clause or an explicitly licensed absolute construction.
8. Use commas around introductory participle clauses and check that the modifier attaches to the intended noun.

### 4.4. Сравнение с близкими конструкциями

| Full clause | Reduced form | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| While Kate was making coffee, she was in the kitchen. | Kate was in the kitchen making coffee. | Simultaneous action |
| Because I felt tired, I went to bed early. | Feeling tired, I went to bed early. | Reason |
| After she had finished her work, she went home. | Having finished her work, she went home. | Earlier completed action |
| The boy who was injured in the accident was taken to hospital. | The boy injured in the accident was taken to hospital. | Reduced passive relative |
| Although the paper was criticised, it was accepted. | Although criticised, the paper was accepted. | Reduced passive adverbial |
| Because the weather was fine, we ate outside. | The weather being fine, we ate outside. | Absolute clause with its own subject |

### 4.5. Примеры с переводом и объяснением

| English | Перевод | Почему это подходит |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Having completed the survey, the researchers began analysing the results.` | Завершив опрос, исследователи приступили к анализу результатов. | Having + V3 marks an action completed before the main action. |
| `Disturbed by the sudden noise, the birds flew away.` | Вспугнутые внезапным шумом, птицы улетели. | Disturbed is a passive participle: the birds received the effect. |
| `The team collected samples to avoid contamination.` | Команда собрала образцы, чтобы избежать загрязнения. | To avoid expresses the purpose of collecting the samples; avoid takes a noun or -ing complement. |
| `Having operated for several hours, the machine finally stopped.` | Проработав несколько часов, машина наконец остановилась. | The machine performed the operation for hours; active perfect participle is appropriate. |

Примеры составлены Speakbase. Они иллюстрируют грамматическую функцию, но не подменяют индивидуальную проверку всех возможных вариантов и исключений.

### 4.6. Исключения, ограничения и границы употребления

- -ing clauses can express time, reason or accompanying action; context determines the relationship.
- Having + V3 marks an earlier event; do not use it automatically for simultaneous actions.
- Having been + V3 marks an earlier passive event; distinguish it from being + V3.
- A reduced relative -ing clause is active; a V3 clause is passive/resultative in the intended pattern.
- The understood subject of an adverbial participle clause normally matches the main-clause subject; otherwise a dangling modifier may result.
- Some participle clauses have their own subject (absolute clauses); do not assume all non-finite clauses are subjectless.
- After prepositions, use -ing. Prepositional to + -ing differs from infinitive to + base verb.
- A conjunction + participle reduction is possible only when the omitted subject is recoverable and the relation is clear.
- Use a full finite clause when the subject, sequence or logical relation could be misread.
- Introductory participle clauses normally take a comma; punctuation does not fix a dangling modifier.

### 4.7. Типичные ошибки русскоязычных студентов

| `Having complete the survey, the researchers began analysing the results.` | `Having completed the survey, the researchers began analysing the results.` | Complete is the base form; perfect participle requires V3. |
| `Disturbing by the sudden noise, the birds flew away.` | `Disturbed by the sudden noise, the birds flew away.` | Disturbing would mean the birds caused the noise/disturbance. |
| `The team collected samples to avoid to contamination.` | `The team collected samples to avoid contamination.` | Avoid takes a noun or -ing complement directly; the extra preposition to is not licensed. |

Русский перевод помогает понять смысл, но не управляет английской формой напрямую. Проверяй роль каждого элемента и грамматическую модель английского предложения.

### 4.12. Краткая памятка

- Purpose: to + base verb.
- Simultaneous/accompanying action or reason: -ing.
- Earlier active event: having + V3; earlier passive event: having been + V3.
- Reduced relative/adverbial passive: V3; active: -ing.
- Preposition + -ing; prepositional to + -ing is not infinitive to.
- Conjunction + participle only when the subject is clear.
- Check subject control; absolute clauses can have their own subject.
- Use a full clause if reduction creates ambiguity or a dangling modifier.
