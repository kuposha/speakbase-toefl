## 4. Полный конспект

### 4.1. Основная идея и функция конструкции

На более высоком уровне Passive Voice сочетается с modal verbs и reporting structures. Be сохраняет нужную форму, а основной глагол остаётся в V3; reporting passive позволяет выдвинуть на первый план утверждение или источник.

Тема этого блока — **Passive with modals and reporting passive**. Измеримая цель: Строить modal passive, impersonal/reporting passive и выбирать информационный фокус

### 4.2. ???????????? ? ?????????????? ??????

**1. Simple passive: be + V3**

- Present simple: **am/is/are + V3** ? *The room is cleaned every day.*
- Past simple: **was/were + V3** ? *The room was cleaned yesterday.*

Use the passive when the affected participant is more important than the agent, or the agent is unknown/irrelevant. Add **by + agent** only when the agent matters.

**2. Perfect passive: have/has/had + been + V3**

- *The trip has been cancelled.*
- *The room had been cleaned before we arrived.*

The auxiliary carries tense/aspect; **been + V3** forms the passive.

**3. Continuous passive: be + being + V3**

- *The room is being cleaned at the moment.*
- *The road was being repaired when we arrived.*

Being marks an action in progress; do not omit it from the continuous passive.

**4. Infinitive and modal passive**

- **to be + V3**: *The room will be cleaned later; something must be done.*
- **to have been + V3**: *The documents seem to have been misplaced.*
- **modal + be + V3**: *The form must be submitted by Friday.*
- **modal + have been + V3**: *The documents should have been sent earlier.*

Choose the infinitive/perfect form by the time relation between the reported event and the reporting point.

**5. Passive with two objects**

- *They gave me a watch.* ? *I was given a watch.*
- *They gave a watch to me.* ? *A watch was given to me.*

With verbs such as give, offer, pay, show and tell, the person is often the more natural passive subject. Check which passive alternatives the verb allows.

**6. Being + V3 and get-passive**

- *I don't like being told what to do.*
- *Nobody got hurt.*

Being + V3 is a gerund-participial passive complement. Get-passive is common in informal speech and often describes an event/change; be-passive is neutral and works in more contexts.

**7. Impersonal/reporting passive**

- *It is said that he is 108.*
- *He is said to be 108.*
- *He is alleged to have stolen a car.*
- *Two people are reported to have been injured.*

Use **It + passive reporting verb + that-clause** or **subject + passive reporting verb + infinitive**. Use a perfect infinitive for an earlier event. The structure reports a claim; it does not confirm it.

**8. Supposed to**

**Be supposed to + infinitive** can mean:
- reported belief: *The film is supposed to be good*;
- intended/arranged expectation: *You are supposed to be on holiday*;
- rule/prohibition: *You're not supposed to park here*.

Use context to distinguish these meanings.

1. Decide whether the focus should be on the agent or the affected participant.
2. Choose be + V3 for simple passive; add have/has/had for perfect or being for continuous.
3. For modal/infinitive forms, decide whether the passive event is simultaneous/current or earlier: be + V3 vs have been + V3.
4. With two-object verbs, choose the passive subject that carries the intended focus and confirm the verb permits the pattern.
5. Use being + V3 after a preposition/verb when a gerund-participial passive is required.
6. Use get-passive mainly for an event/change and informal style; use be-passive for neutral/general contexts.
7. For reporting, choose it + passive + that-clause or subject + passive reporting verb + infinitive; use perfect infinitive for an earlier event.
8. For supposed to, identify whether the meaning is reported belief, arrangement/expectation or rule.

### 4.6. ??????????, ??????????? ? ??????? ????????????

- Not every verb is passivised naturally; check whether it takes an object and whether the passive preserves the intended meaning.
- Be + V3 can describe a state as well as an event; context distinguishes the two.
- Continuous passive requires **being + V3**; perfect passive requires **been + V3**.
- With two-object verbs, both passive orders are not always equally natural or available.
- Get-passive is more informal and event-focused; it is not a universal replacement for be.
- **Being born** is not the normal expression for the event of birth; use **was/were born** for the event and **are born** for general facts.
- Reporting passive expresses what is said/believed/reported, not a verified fact.
- **Supposed to** has several meanings: reported belief, intended arrangement/expectation, and rule/prohibition. Interpret by context.
- In a reporting passive, to have + V3 marks an earlier event; do not use it merely because the reporting verb is in the present.

### 4.7. Типичные ошибки русскоязычных студентов

| `All applications must submit by Friday.` | `All applications must be submitted by Friday.` | Submit is active base form; the subject applications receive the action. |
| `The missing documents should have sent earlier.` | `The missing documents should have been sent earlier.` | Sent omits been and is not the complete passive sequence. |
| `It is widely believed that this species is become extinct.` | `It is widely believed that this species has become extinct.` | Is become is not a valid present perfect form. |

Русский перевод помогает понять смысл, но не управляет английской формой напрямую. Проверяй роль каждого элемента и грамматическую модель английского предложения.
