## 4. Полный конспект

### 4.1. Основная идея и функция конструкции

Advanced concession and correlative structures establish contrast, qualification and parallel relationships between propositions. They often require balanced syntax and carefully controlled inversion.

Тема этого блока — **Advanced concession and correlative structures**. Измеримая цель: Строить сложные контрастные и параллельные конструкции, включая inversion after not only

### 4.2. Формирование и грамматические модели

**1. Concession with although, though and even though**

- **Although/though + clause** introduces a fact that contrasts with the main clause: *Although the sample was small, the results were consistent.*
- **Even though** adds emphasis to the factual contrast.
- **Though** can also appear at the end as a comment: *The results were consistent. The sample was small, though.*
- **Despite/in spite of + noun/-ing** do not take a finite clause directly: *Despite the small sample... / In spite of having limited data...*

**2. Even if, while/whilst and whereas**

- **Even if** introduces a possible or hypothetical concession: *Even if the next trial succeeds, the mechanism will remain unclear.*
- **While/whilst** can mean 'although' or introduce a contrast between two facts; **whereas** explicitly contrasts two situations: *The first method is cheaper, whereas the second is more reliable.*
- Do not confuse **while** meaning 'during the time that' with **while** meaning 'although/whereas'.

**3. Not necessarily**

**Not necessarily** means 'not always' or 'not in every case'; it does not mean 'certainly not'. It qualifies a generalisation: *A longer test is not necessarily a more accurate test.*

**4. Correlative structures and parallelism**

- **Not only...but also** adds a second, often surprising point. Keep the two elements grammatically parallel.
- **Neither...nor** links two negative alternatives. When the pair forms the subject (*Neither the explanation nor the alternative account fits*), use normal subject–verb order; agreement commonly follows the nearer subject in formal usage. When **neither** or **nor** introduces a separate clause (*Neither did the first test confirm it, nor did the second*), use inversion after the fronted negative adverb. Do not apply inversion mechanically to every neither...nor pair.
- **Whether...or** introduces alternatives or uncertainty.
- **As...as** expresses equality; use the base adjective/adverb between the two as markers.
- **No sooner...than** and **hardly/scarcely...when/before** express that one event follows another quickly; their inversion patterns belong to C1-01 and are cross-referenced rather than retaught here.
- **Rather than** can coordinate parallel nouns, -ing forms or infinitives; keep the form consistent.

**5. Inversion for emphasis**

- Initial **not only**: *Not only did the policy reduce costs, but it also improved access.*
- Initial **neither/nor** can trigger inversion when it introduces a clause: *Neither did the first test confirm the result, nor did the second.* A subject pair (*Neither the first nor the second test confirmed it*) keeps normal order.
- **So + adjective/adverb + auxiliary/be + subject + that-clause** and **such + be + subject + that-clause** are advanced inversion patterns covered in C1-01; recognise them here but do not confuse them with concession.

Use the auxiliary required by tense and verb. If the clause has a lexical verb in the past simple, use did + subject + base verb. Do not invert when the correlative phrase is not fronted in the relevant pattern.

**6. Discourse connectors**

Connectors such as **however, nevertheless, consequently, therefore, moreover, in addition, otherwise** show contrast, result, addition or alternative consequence. Choose the connector by logical relation, not by sentence length. Punctuation depends on whether the connector links clauses or introduces a sentence-level comment.

### 4.3. Как выбрать подходящую форму

1. Decide whether the relation is factual concession, hypothetical concession, contrast, addition, alternative or result.
2. Use although/though/even though + finite clause; use despite/in spite of + noun or -ing.
3. Use even if for a hypothetical condition; use whereas/while for contrast between two facts.
4. Use not necessarily to qualify a generalisation, not to deny it categorically.
5. Identify both halves of a correlative pair and keep them parallel.
6. Check the exact structure: clause-initial not only and nor may require inversion; a neither...nor subject pair does not. No sooner/hardly patterns are cross-referenced to C1-01.
7. Choose a discourse connector that accurately expresses contrast, result, addition or alternative consequence.
8. Check subject–verb agreement and punctuation, especially with neither/nor and sentence-initial connectors.

### 4.4. Сравнение с близкими конструкциями

Not only did the policy reduce costs, but it also improved access. Whether the cause was internal or external remains unclear. The results were as significant as expected.

| Что проверяем | Вопрос для выбора |
|---|---|
| Форма | Какие элементы обязательны и в каком порядке они стоят? |
| Значение | Что именно меняется: время, длительность, реальность, степень уверенности или фокус? |
| Контекст | Что уже известно собеседнику и каков регистр высказывания? |
| Проверка | Если заменить форму близкой конструкцией, изменится ли смысл? Объясни, почему. |

| English | Перевод | Почему это подходит |

| `Not only did the researchers repeat the procedure, but they also documented every stage.` | Исследователи не только повторили процедуру, но и задокументировали каждый этап. | Initial Not only triggers inversion; Past Simple needs did + subject + base verb. |

| `Neither the proposed explanation nor the alternative account fits the data.` | Ни предлагаемое объяснение, ни альтернативная версия не соответствуют данным. | Neither…nor introduces two negative alternatives. |

### 4.6. Исключения, ограничения и границы употребления

- Although/though/even though take a finite clause; despite/in spite of take a noun phrase or -ing clause, not a finite clause directly.
- Even if is hypothetical; even though presents the contrast as a fact.
- While is ambiguous between time and contrast; use whereas when an explicit contrast is needed.
- Not necessarily means 'not always', not 'never' or 'certainly not'.
- Correlative structures require parallel grammatical forms; avoid mixing a noun phrase with an unrelated clause.
- Initial not only can trigger subject–auxiliary inversion; a neither/nor subject pair does not. Nor at the start of a new negative clause can trigger inversion. Mid-sentence correlative forms do not automatically trigger it.
- Agreement with neither/nor and either/or can depend on the nearer subject and style; rewrite if the result sounds awkward.
- No sooner...than and hardly/scarcely...when/before are separate fixed pairs; their inversion is covered in C1-01.
- Connectors express logical relations; therefore is not a synonym of however, and moreover does not introduce a concession.

### 4.7. Типичные ошибки русскоязычных студентов

| `Not only the researchers repeated the procedure, but they also documented every stage.` | `Not only did the researchers repeat the procedure, but they also documented every stage.` | The researchers repeated keeps statement order and misses required inversion. |
| `The policy was designed not only to reduce costs and also to improve access.` | `The policy was designed not only to reduce costs but also to improve access.` | And does not complete the paired construction. |
| `Neither the proposed explanation or the alternative account fits the data.` | `Neither the proposed explanation nor the alternative account fits the data.` | Or does not complete the correlative pair after neither. |

Русский перевод помогает понять смысл, но не управляет английской формой напрямую. Проверяй роль каждого элемента и грамматическую модель английского предложения.

### 4.8. Произношение и восприятие на слух

Concessive subordinators are usually unstressed and closely linked to the clause they introduce. Contrastive stress may fall on the contrasting phrase after **whereas** or on the element qualified by **not necessarily**. In spoken correlative structures, keep both coordinated elements perceptually balanced. Phonetic transcriptions and audio examples still require dictionary-audio verification.

Для аудирования распознавай слабые формы вспомогательных глаголов, окончания и границы смысловых групп. Не ориентируйся только на чтение по буквам.

### 4.12. Краткая памятка

- Although/though/even though + finite clause; despite/in spite of + noun/-ing.
- Even if = hypothetical concession; whereas/while = contrast; not necessarily = not always.
- Keep not only?but also, neither?nor, whether?or and as?as parallel.
- Initial not only/neither/nor/no sooner/hardly/scarcely can trigger inversion.
- No sooner...than; hardly/scarcely...when/before.
- Choose however/nevertheless (contrast), therefore/consequently (result), moreover/in addition (addition), otherwise (alternative consequence) by logic.
