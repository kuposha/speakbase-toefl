## 4. Полный конспект

### 4.1. Основная идея и функция конструкции

Nominalisation converts verbs or adjectives into nouns, allowing actions, processes and qualities to function as compact topics in academic discourse. It can improve cohesion but may also make prose dense or obscure agency.

Тема этого блока — **Nominalisation and academic style — introduction**. Измеримая цель: Преобразовывать действия/качества в noun phrases и управлять формальностью

### 4.2. Формирование и грамматическая модель
**1. Verb-to-noun formation**
Nominalisation forms a noun from a verb. Common patterns include:
- **-tion/-sion:** expand -> expansion, decide -> decision, inform -> information, apply -> application, analyse -> analysis.
- **-ment:** develop -> development, improve -> improvement, achieve -> achievement.
- **-ance/-ence:** perform -> performance, differ -> difference, exist -> existence.
- **-al:** arrive -> arrival, refuse -> refusal.
These suffixes are tendencies, not fully productive rules. Learn the established noun form and its spelling rather than guessing.

**2. Adjective-to-noun formation**
- **-ity/-ty:** accurate -> accuracy, possible -> possibility, safe -> safety, efficient -> efficiency.
- **-ness:** effective -> effectiveness, aware -> awareness.
- **-th:** strong -> strength, wide -> width, high -> height.
Spelling may change; some forms are irregular. Check the dictionary form when unsure.

**3. Noun phrase structure**
A nominalised head noun can take a determiner, pre-modifiers and complements: the rapid expansion of the city; a significant improvement in efficiency; the decision by the committee to postpone the vote; the claim that the policy failed. Identify the head noun and its complement.
- A verb object often becomes an of-phrase (analyse the data -> analysis of the data), but dependent prepositions are lexical (response to, increase in, explanation of).
- An agent can be shown with a possessive or by-phrase (the decision by the committee; the implementation of the policy by the agency). Keep the agent when it matters.

**4. Countability and irregular forms**
Some nominalisations are uncountable (information, evidence, research, accuracy, efficiency); others are countable (a decision, an analysis, several analyses). Check articles and number. Common irregular plurals include analysis/analyses, crisis/crises, criterion/criteria, phenomenon/phenomena.

**5. Nominalisation, gerunds and participles**
A gerund in -ing can name an activity (Measuring the samples took time), while a lexical nominalisation packages it as a concept/process (The measurement of the samples took time). Not every -ing form is a nominalisation. Distinguish a noun head (the expansion) from a participle/adjective (the expanding city).

**6. Nominal style vs verbal style**
Nominal style can create cohesion and compact topics, especially when a process is already known. A finite verb often makes agency, time and cause clearer. Avoid long chains of abstract nouns; do not nominalise simply to sound formal.
### 4.3. Как выбрать подходящую форму
1. Decide whether the action/quality should become the topic or whether a finite verb is clearer.
2. Identify the source verb/adjective and choose its established noun form; do not guess the suffix.
3. Build the noun phrase: determiner + pre-modifier + head noun + complement where needed.
4. Check countability, article and number (an analysis; several analyses; information without a plural -s).
5. Preserve the original relationship: identify the agent, object and dependent preposition; use an of-/to-/in-phrase or possessive only where licensed.
6. Check whether the -ing form is a gerund/activity noun, a participle/adjective or whether a lexical noun is more suitable.
7. Re-read the sentence for clarity. If nominalisation hides the agent or makes the sentence dense, return to a finite verb.
### 4.4. Сравнение с близкими конструкциями

The company expanded rapidly → the rapid expansion of the company. Researchers analysed the data → the analysis of the data.

| Что проверяем | Вопрос для выбора |
|---|---|
| Форма | Какие элементы обязательны и в каком порядке они стоят? |
| Значение | Что именно меняется: время, длительность, реальность, степень уверенности или фокус? |
| Контекст | Что уже известно собеседнику и каков регистр высказывания? |
| Проверка | Если заменить форму близкой конструкцией, изменится ли смысл? Объясни, почему. |

### 4.5. Примеры с переводом и объяснением

| English | Перевод | Почему это подходит |
|---|---|---|
| The rapid expansion of the city increased housing costs. | Быстрый рост города привёл к увеличению стоимости жилья. | Expansion is a countable singular process noun after the adjective rapid; the noun phrase has the city as its of-complement. |
| The researchers analysed the results carefully -> Their careful analysis of the results was detailed. | Их тщательный анализ результатов был детальным. | Analysis is the noun form of analyse; the object results becomes an of-phrase, and careful becomes a pre-modifier. |
| The gradual implementation of the policy took two years. | Постепенное внедрение политики заняло два года. | Implementation names the process; the policy becomes its of-complement. This sentence illustrates the nominalised phrase, not a strict tense-preserving paraphrase. |
| Accurate measurement is essential -> The accuracy of the measurements is essential. | Точность измерений необходима. | Accuracy is the noun form of accurate; it functions as the head of the subject noun phrase. |

Примеры составлены Speakbase. Они иллюстрируют грамматическую функцию, но не подменяют индивидуальную проверку всех возможных вариантов и исключений.

### 4.6. Исключения, ограничения и границы употребления

Do not nominalise every verb. Excessive noun phrases can hide the agent and reduce readability; prefer a finite verb when it makes the relationship clearer. Use an agentive possessive or by-phrase when the actor matters.

Не превращай наблюдение в абсолютное правило, если существуют варианты BrE/AmE, различия регистра или контекстные значения. В спорных случаях фиксируй конкретную норму и источник.

### 4.7. Типичные ошибки русскоязычных студентов
| Incorrect | Correct | Why |
|---|---|---|
| The rapid expand of the city increased housing costs. | The rapid expansion of the city increased housing costs. | Expand is a verb; expansion is the noun head required after rapid. |
| Their careful analyse of the results was detailed. | Their careful analysis of the results was detailed. | Analyse is the verb; analysis is the noun form required after the possessive + adjective. |
| The gradual implement of the policy took two years. | The gradual implementation of the policy took two years. | Implement is a verb; implementation is the noun naming the process. |
| The accurate of the measurements is essential. | The accuracy of the measurements is essential. | Accurate is an adjective; accuracy is the corresponding noun. |
### 4.8. Произношение и восприятие на слух

Listen for stress shifts and syllable changes in noun forms such as analyse/analysis, expand/expansion and accurate/accuracy. In connected speech, identify the noun head and its dependent phrase rather than treating the whole nominal group as one word.

Для аудирования распознавай слабые формы вспомогательных глаголов, окончания и границы смысловых групп. Не ориентируйся только на чтение по буквам.

### 4.12. Краткая памятка

- **Фокус:** Nominalisation and academic style — introduction.
- **Базовая модель:** Verb → noun (analyse → analysis, expand → expansion). Adjective → noun (accurate → accuracy). The noun can take determiners, modifiers and of-phrases: the rapid expansion of the city.
- **Ключ выбора:** Определи, нужно ли представить действие как процесс/концепцию или сохранить активный verb sentence. Проверь article, number, complement and clarity after nominalisation.
- **Ограничение:** Do not nominalise every verb. Excessive noun phrases can hide the agent and reduce readability; prefer a finite verb when it makes the relationship clearer.
- При редактировании выбирай наиболее ясную и точную форму, а не самую сложную.
